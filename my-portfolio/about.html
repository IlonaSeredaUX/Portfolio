Hi, I'm Ilona, a Product and UX/UI Designer

Originally from Ukraine (Dnipro, Korsun-Shevchenkivskyi, Kyiv), I lived in Montreal, Canada (Oui, je parle français), and now I live in Portugal (Estou a aprender português).

Currently I'm building AI-powered wellness app FemEntity. In my work, I'm focusing on bridging the gap between user and business, while facilitating efficient workflows and cross-team collaboration.

I work remotely from Portugal (UTC+0) and collaborate comfortably across European and Eastern Time (ET) time zones.

I have hands-on experience across end-to-end design processes, from research and problem framing to redesign initiatives and design system development.

Always open to meaningful collaborations — let's connect on Linkedin!
