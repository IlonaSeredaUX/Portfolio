About me

Hello! I’m Ilona, a Product and UX/UI Designer. Originally from Ukraine, I created this portfolio inspired by the artwork of Heorhiy Narbut, a Ukrainian graphic designer who represents Art Nouveau.

As a designer, I enjoy combining visual design with user experience and research, making AI my co-pilot while keeping the human at the center of the design process.
